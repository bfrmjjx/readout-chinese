# ReadOut Chinese: Privacy Policy

[ภาษาไทย](privacy-th.md) · Last updated: 4 October 2026

ReadOut Chinese (อ่านจีนออก) is a Chinese reading and dictionary app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

- **We do not collect your data.** There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
- **Your words, notes, books and scans stay on your device.** The app has no server of its own.
- **Some optional buttons open other websites or apps** (for example Google Translate). When you use them, the word or text you chose goes to that service, under its own privacy policy.

## What stays on your device

The app stores these only on your iPhone or iPad:

- words you save, your notes, your review progress and your settings;
- book pages you scan, photos you choose, PDFs you open and stories you type or paste, and the text read from them;
- pages you send to the app from other apps with the Share button.

We cannot see any of this. If your device backs itself up to iCloud or a computer, Apple's backup may include this app's data, under Apple's privacy policy. If you make a backup file in the app and share it, you choose where that file goes.

## Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera and your photos only so it can read the Chinese on a page you choose. Reading the text happens on your device. Your pictures are not uploaded anywhere by the app. You can turn this access off at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

## Translation, reading aloud and Apple Intelligence

Translation uses Apple's built-in translation, read-aloud uses the voices built into iOS, and some features use Apple Intelligence on your device. Apple may download language files to your device the first time. These features are provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the app does not send your text to us.

## Optional links to other services

These only happen when you tap them:

- **Web dictionaries and search** (such as Google, Google Translate, Bing Translator, Baidu, MDBG, YouGlish, Youdao, Sogou, Purple Culture, or a website you add yourself): the word you are looking at is sent to that website, which opens in Safari.
- **"Translate on the web"**: the Chinese text of your page is sent to Google Translate, which opens in Safari.
- **"Ask an AI"** (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini): the app copies your page's text to the clipboard and opens that service. Nothing is sent until you paste it there yourself.
- **"Report a mistake"**: opens your email app with a message to us. If you send it, we receive your email address and what you wrote, and use it only to reply and fix the dictionary.

Those websites and apps have their own privacy policies and may use cookies. We do not control them and receive nothing from them.

## Purchases

Premium is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your card or Apple ID details. The app checks with Apple on your device whether you own Premium.

## Children

The app is made for learners of all ages, including children using a parent's device. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases go through Apple, so parents can use Apple's Screen Time and Ask to Buy to approve them. Parents may wish to supervise the optional website links above.

## Your rights

Laws such as Thailand's PDPA, the EU and UK GDPR and Singapore's PDPA give you rights over your personal data. Because the app keeps your data only on your device, you control it directly: you can delete any item in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. If you emailed us, you can ask us to delete that email.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
